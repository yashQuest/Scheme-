WHO WE ARE

Bridging the gap between citizens & opportunities.

Government schemes can provide valuable support in education, healthcare, employment, agriculture, housing and many other areas.

However, finding the right scheme and understanding its eligibility requirements can sometimes be difficult.

OurScheme was created to make that process simpler by bringing useful scheme information together in a clean and easy-to-use platform.

OUR MISSION

Information should be easy for everyone.
